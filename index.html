Service / Award

I am an ARC Discovery Early Career Researcher Awardee (DECRA Fellow) and a Senior Lecturer (US Associate Professor equivalent) at the School of Computing Technologies, RMIT University, Melbourne, Australia. I earned my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science at RMIT University in 2022.

I am the recipient of the ARC DECRA Award 2026, the sole Best Paper Award of ESORICS 2021, the RMIT HDR Research Prize 2023. My work has appeared in prestigious venues in computer security, such as USENIX Security, NDSS, IEEE TDSC, TIFS. I have served on the technical program committee (TPC member) of USENIX Security, EuroS&P, ASIACCS, and the program co-chair of LAMPS at CCS 2025. I also serve as an Associate Editor for IEEE TSC.
